QATSEL™Quiet Ambient Techno Soul of Eternal Life

Four wallpapers built around one anonymous figure made of light: a ghost with a sky for skin, a body in transit, a hand that holds the signal, and a soul at rest printed in ultramarine. Everything on this page runs on the same engine that rendered the files, live, at your screen's resolution.

The four

Phone plates · 1320 × 2868 · tap a plate to glitch it

IGHOST

ambient, ghostly

The figure stands on a line of horizon light with a sky for skin. Stars rise slowly through the body, spiked stars hang around it and a thin anamorphic line crosses at head height. The edges dissolve like breath on glass.

IIILLUSION

break the illusion

Arms open inside streams of light moving at three depths. The core is overexposed, petals of light fall through the halo and the chromatic fringes widen toward the frame edge.

IIIWIELDER

tech cyber wielder

An open hand drawn as a CRT raster. The scanlines bend over each finger, the bones show through like an x-ray and electric filaments run along the contour. A light pulses in the palm.

IVSAVED

capable of achieving all dreams

The same light printed as ultramarine halftone on off-white paper. A floating figure in solid ink with stars knocked out, light smears as dot columns, a slipped second plate. The glitch tears the paper and shows the night underneath.

The shared system

What ties the collective together

Light over a dot matrix

Every design is light, and under the light sits a dot matrix. When the glitch hits, quantized bands tear sideways and expose the matrix for about half a second before the image settles. In IV the order flips: the print is the surface and the night is underneath.

Lens, grain, raster

Radial chromatic aberration opens toward the edges, frosted grain sits on every gradient and a faint CRT raster runs across the field. Each effect is scaled to the screen's short side, so a phone and a 34-inch ultrawide carry the same texture.

Type you find late

An off-white label in the manner of a garment tag: a bold wordmark, an italic serif line, a mono index, vertical mono lines on the edges. It sits low enough to clear the lock-screen clock and the Dock.

Install

Per device

iPhone and iPad, Live Photo

  1. Each Live Photo is a pair, .JPG + .MOV with the same name. Both files carry the same content identifier.
  2. On a Mac, drag both files of a pair into Photos together. Photos merges them into one Live Photo and iCloud syncs it to the phone.
  3. On the phone: Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos, pick the Live Photo and keep Live Photo on. The motion plays when you wake the screen.
  4. Phone only, no Mac: open the .MOV in a converter such as intoLive and save it as a Live Photo.

Duo pair

  1. Set the -L file on one phone and the -R file on the other.
  2. Lay the phones side by side: the horizon, the streams and the reaching hands continue across the gap.

Galaxy Z Fold 8

  1. Copy the MP4 loops to the phone.
  2. Settings → Wallpaper and style → Change wallpapers → Lock screen → Gallery, then choose the video. Samsung accepts clips up to 15 s; these are 10 s.
  3. The cover screen and main screen are set separately. Use the matching file for each.

MacBook

  1. Still: System Settings → Wallpaper → Add Photo, and pick the file for your model.
  2. Live: macOS has no native video wallpaper. Open wallpaper-engine/QATSEL/index.html in Plash to run the engine live at native resolution, or play the MP4 loop with a video-wallpaper app.

Wallpaper Engine, 3440 × 1440

  1. Wallpaper Engine → Open Wallpaper → Open from File, and select wallpaper-engine/QATSEL/index.html.
  2. The properties panel sets the design, glitch interval and intensity, motion speed, typography, audio-reactive light and render scale.
  3. Click the desktop to break the illusion on demand.

The Live Photo pairs follow Apple's pairing format and were checked with ExifTool. They were not tested on a physical iPhone. If Photos imports the pair as two separate files, use the converter route.
